Emily Bei Cheng

Hi, I’m Emily Bei Cheng, an artist and engineer based in San Francisco. I explore life at photo pace and share what I find through words and images. My stories tend to start in unlikely places: a bivvy on a mountain summit, a mural in the Paris Catacombs, a wrong turn in the Gates of the Arctic. I became ultra-curious in 2025 and I wrote about my experience in my essay reflecting on the 2025 Japanese Odyssey. I love exploring places, but I’m equally curious about people, and I’m happiest when I’m getting to know what makes someone tick.
